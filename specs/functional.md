# Functional Requirements

## Trend Research

As an Agent, I must fetch trending topics across supported platforms within a defined timeframe.

## Content Generation

As an Agent, I must generate platform-appropriate content based on selected trends.

## Validation & Safety

As an Agent, I must validate all outputs for quality, safety, and policy compliance before publishing.

## Human Review

As an Operator, I must review sensitive or low-confidence outputs before approval.

## Financial Operations

As the System, I must enforce budget limits and human approval for all wallet actions.

## Monitoring

As an Operator, I must view task status, audit trails, and performance metrics.
